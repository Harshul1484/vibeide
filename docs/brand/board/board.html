What we heard

The brief

VibeIDE is a VS Code-style IDE for Android with a real Linux sandbox on the device and an AI agent whose edits you review before they land. The mark should feel like an AI-era product (a friendly, minimal sign) while staying in the app's own palette.

capablefriendlyin flowin control

Success: minimal and ownable like the AI marks the team admires, built on the app's #0078D4 accent, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour and reversed.

  • Built entirely on the app's accent blue #0078D4
  • Arc-and-capsule geometry: one width, one radius, 120° symmetry
  • Heavier small-size cut keeps the gaps open at 16 px
